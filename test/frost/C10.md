## Review fixes (21 October 2026)

Fixes for the review of #20 and tbtc-v2 #14:

- **Reservation finality (#20).** The planner and the grant-time view
  accepted a reservation present only at the head, so a shallow
  reorganization after broadcast could remove it. Both now require the
  reservation open at the head and present the reveal-finality depth earlier.
  The K-06 fixture anchors plans on `readyBlock`, the block holding both the
  reveal and the reservation. New unit cases: a reservation first present
  at 99 or 100 (head 100, finality 2) is refused, and one at 98 is accepted.
  These cases fail without the fix.
- **Credit binding and drain after a main change (#14).** Fixed in tbtc-v2.
  See `C10-BOUNDARIES.md`, "Review fixes".

## Adversarial review fixes (23 October 2026)

The adversarial review of #20 and tbtc-v2 #14 confirmed 20 findings, which
are all fixed here. tbtc-v2 changes are described in `C10-BOUNDARIES.md`,
"Adversarial review fixes". On the host:

- **Latest commitment event.** The legacy moving-funds proposal takes the
  most recent `MovingFundsCommitmentSubmitted` event whose targets match the
  stored commitment, and the SPV maintainer proves each wallet's latest one.
  A C-10 drain release clears the commitment and the source commits again.
  Before this fix, the second event stopped every proposal for that wallet.
  The generator still recommits to legacy targets after a release; choosing
  FROST targets is K-09/K-11 host scope.
- **Reservation eligibility.** `ReserveDeposit` reserves only a deposit the
  planner could sweep. Its funding output must be unspent, and median time
  past plus `RefundMargin` must fall before the locktime. Otherwise it
  refuses with kind `funding-spent` or `refund-margin` and submits nothing.
- **Lost reply and lost race.** After a failed submission the reread keeps
  the record and reports `already-swept` or `already-reserved`.
- **Reservation seam.** Deposits are now counted in aggregate, so the host
  reads `frostDepositReserved` from the Bridge. The deposit record is 24
  words, with C-10's `reservedAt` last.
- **Planner depth.** The planner test also requires a reservation present at
  depth 98 (finality 2, anchor 100) to be planned.
