- D25 (a), tux 2026-11-09: a host restored from its saved wallet record
  quarantines on a removed or replaced approval. The restore clause of
  P-K03-INCONSISTENCY-QUARANTINE (`RESTORE-SCOPE-2026-11-06.md` section 3) is
  therefore the owner's rule, no longer only proposed.

  one-restart bound are adopted into the local KC-VER rev 02 amendment
  (not pushed; no tuxxy fork of the spec repository). The specification pins
  stay at `d45afda`.

## 3. Model change (correspondence row replaced)

`RESTORE-SCOPE-2026-11-06.md` section 2.1, row `Watch(n)` on a restored node,
is replaced by:

| Finite operation | Go | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Restart(n)`, key present | `Restore` up to the `after-key-reload` pause, now including the approval seeding | `seen` is set for the restored node (it was cleared before) |
| `Watch(n)` on a restored node | restored monitor with approval knowledge seeded from the saved record | a removed approval quarantines through the same guard as on `Run` (`(seen | watched) & n` and `chain != 1`) |

Predicates and witnesses are unchanged; the restore clause of
P-K03-INCONSISTENCY-QUARANTINE is now expected to pass.

**Weakening 11 (new, target P-K03-INCONSISTENCY-QUARANTINE).** The restored
process does not seed its approval knowledge (the pre-#135 behaviour). It
must break that predicate and no other claim predicate. It shows the pass
depends on the seeding.

**Boundary traces.** `B-RESTORE-READY-RETURN-REMOVAL` now ends at the
`Watch(0)` that quarantines (it used to continue to `Complete(0)`, which the
seeded model no longer offers; first model run stopped there, kept as
`model-attempt-1-boundary-trace-panic/`). New `B-RESTORE-RELOAD-REMOVAL`: a
restored node paused at `after-key-reload` reads the approval removed and
quarantines.

**Replay correspondence (added after the first replay, classified before
repair).** The first replay with the new model (kept as `attempt-2/`) failed
one trace, `P-K03-INCONSISTENCY-QUARANTINE-control-11`: the model quarantined
the restored node at `after-key-reload`, while Go, resumed, first emitted
`before-readiness-collect`. Finding F-V03-R3, class: replay harness correspondence gap, not an
implementation or model defect. The monitor cancels the executor while the
main goroutine is paused; Go reaches the outcome at its next context-aware
step (as recorded for F-V03-R2), and `after-key-reload` is followed directly
by `before-readiness-collect` with no step between (`executor.go` `ready`).
The harness's `Watch` step now passes that one hook, only from
`after-key-reload`, and fails unless the key, reload, wallet record and DKG
status it reports are identical to those at `after-key-reload`.
