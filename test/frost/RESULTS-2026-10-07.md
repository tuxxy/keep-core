## Failures retained and corrected

Earlier diagnostics remain beside the final logs. They are not counted as pass
evidence. In particular, a race run safely rejected a registry snapshot whose
block differed from the requested head. The final adapter discards that response
and allows at most five read attempts. Reorgs and other failures are not retried;
a persistent mismatch quarantines the attempt. This cannot resume a DKG or
reissue a worker command.

The contract now exposes one atomic epoch view. Fixture block jumps serialize
node views, each chain has a unique ID, and deadline tests stop and join the
miner before setting an exact boundary. The pre-readiness crash hook rejects
buffered delivery after killing the worker. It no longer forwards a readiness
message that was already buffered by the host after the requested crash point.
Earlier binding, compilation and fixture diagnostics are retained as well.

## Review structure and limits

One milestone owns each review. SF-01/K-01 own their candidate regressions;
SF-03 owns capacity regressions; K-02 owns store regressions and the bounded
host model/replay. The prior component PRs are superseded, with history retained.
K-03 has one cross-linked PR in each affected repository. No PR is merged.

The Stateright exploration remains the earlier bounded K-02 result. Replaying
its traces on this source is useful regression evidence, not a K-03 model,
refinement proof or proof of the Go implementation. No new formal exploration
or mutation campaign is claimed. K-09 retains the final lifecycle campaign.

The local policy N=3/t=2/r=2, reserve and block windows are test choices.
D-01 distribution, D-02/D-11 production group and abort policy, D-09 runtime,
storage/fence and finality assumptions, SF-03 capacity and C-07 primitive
qualification remain open. These C-01/C-02/C-03 contracts provide the inactive
support required for this local exit; they do not finish every production
contract gate. There is no FROST Live transition, active selection, deposit
admission, funding or production deployment. K-04 onward has not been implemented.
