## Review fixes (19 October 2026)

Fixes for the review of #19 and tbtc-v2 #13:

- The self-transfer fee cap no longer reads the live parameter. Each
  incoming output records `movedFundsSweepTxMaxTotalFee` when it is created;
  the defense checks that, and the host plans an incoming sweep within the
  smaller of the live and recorded caps (`TestIncomingSweepUsesRecordedFeeCap`
  here, the cap-lowering regression in `Frost.Fraud`).
- A heartbeat round that started and lost its outcome ends the claim window.
- A claim retained at a nonce is reused on retry; another proposal at that
  nonce is refused.
- The defender and timeout notifier continue past a challenge they cannot
  settle and report every error.

Each fix has a unit regression and a guard control.
