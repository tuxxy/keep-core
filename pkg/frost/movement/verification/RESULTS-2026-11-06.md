## Profiles and reductions

The bound is the V-08 bound. Two reductions, both recorded before the
evidence run:

1. **Profile split.** A first combined model (before the quotients below)
   exceeded the 20 GiB limit (about 33.7 GiB at 131 s, unfinished; stopped by
   hand, `sizing.json`). The model is split by predicate family. The
   movement profile has every movement and incoming-sweep step (plan, claim,
   grant, signature, broadcast, abandon) and no closure, challenge or late
   credit. The closure profile has the closure library, challenges and
   defenses, late credits and key deletion, with each movement or sweep
   signature as one host step whose guards are the authority and the prevout
   check. All five properties are checked in both. **Not explored:** closure
   steps interleaved with the internals of a signing attempt. A combined
   profile is not implemented.
2. **Quotients.** Only the current round's claim flag is kept (a past round
   can never be claimed again); `released` is cleared from tick 3 (it only
   enables challenges, which open before tick 3); movement and incoming
   escalation are left out of the closure profile (they change no C-09 row
   the closure predicates read and only remove sweep proposals).

Growth of the closure claim model: 2,502,384 / 7,219,216 / 14,341,294 states
at one / two / three ticks after the quotients.

## Findings

- **F-V08-1, implementation defect (candidate).** An Escalated incoming
  output is never proposed for a sweep on its own. The proposal finder
  (`tbtcpg.MovedFundsSweepTask.findFrostIncomingOutput`) and the follower
  validator (`validateFrostMovedFundsSweepProposal`) both require
  `IsFrostIncomingPending` (`state == IncomingPending`,
  `pkg/chain/ethereum/frost_movement.go:474-484`). C-06 keeps the output
  sweepable and counted in row 2, and the K-07 planner and authority accept
  it. `notifyMovedFundsSweepTimeout` is permissionless, so after
  `movedFundsSweepTimeout` anyone can escalate a pending output; the target
  then cannot close, and the output is swept only if another pending output
  of the same target is proposed. Safety predicates still hold (escalated is
  an allowed state); the specified recovery (BOUNDARIES reading 1: the
  target's own sweep) is unreachable for an escalated-only target. Trace:
  the claim run's missing witness and the probe trace. Scratch reproduction
  (not committed): `TestV08ReproEscalatedIncomingIsProposed` fails for the
  escalated output, and `TestV08ReproEscalatedOnlyPlannedAndAuthorized`
  passes (`findings.json`, `F-V08-1-repro-run.log`). Not fixed.
- **O-V08-1, replay limitation.** All hooks are unexported and unset;
  executor, lock and maintainer hooks cannot be reached without production
  changes, so those steps are modeled only.
- **O-V08-2, scope note.** `movement.Closure` and
  `spv.FrostMovementMaintainer` have no production caller (D17: keeper work).
- **O-V08-3, tooling note.** Witness paths are not minimal.

## Assumptions

A-V08-1 (a host crash loses only volatile K-07 state; V-01), A-V08-2 (a stale
image quarantines until reconciliation; V-01), A-K07-MOVEMENT-VIEW and
A-K07-CLOSURE-LEDGER (`BOUNDARIES.md`), immutable C-01 descriptors, one
Bitcoin spend per output and no mempool eviction. The C-06 sweep main-input
rule is not modeled (an over-approximation of the contract): INCOMING-ONCE
holds on the consumption key alone.
