## Review fixes (19 October 2026)

Fixes for the review of #18 and tbtc-v2 #10 to #12. The runbook was rerun on
the fixed heads (tbtc-v2 C-09 `677da343`): every stage passed again, with the
named tests 4/4, the controls 8/8 killed, 392 contract tests, the K-06 named
tests 5/5, and units, race and vet clean, in 2 min 34 s. The evidence is in
`implementation/K-07/local-2026-10-19/review-run`.

- The incoming sweep authority matches records by outpoint key, not by
  position. Log order no longer refuses a valid sweep.
- A committed movement takes over a main that an aborted K-06 sweep still
  holds, only with the authority's evidence of a finalized, unproven
  commitment naming that main. A sweep never takes a movement's main.
- Deposits no longer expire and reveals never block movement or closure. A
  late sweep of a real deposit reopens a `Closing` or `Closed` wallet into
  `MovingFunds` in a new period. The host dropped `ExpiredAt`, the expiry
  notice and the pending-reveals closure bit (bit 16 is unused).
- The drain commitment keeps the legacy hash. `TestCrossSchemeDrainAndSweep`
  now passes it through the real `WalletProposalValidator`, and checks it
  equals the hash keep-core's proposal generator computes.
- The host plans and authorizes a movement within the commitment's `maxFee`,
  never the current `movingFundsTxMaxTotalFee`. A cap raised after the
  commitment no longer yields a fee that the proof and the C-08 defense
  reject (`TestMovementUsesCommittedFeeCap`, `TestMovementCommittedFeeCap`).
  Rerun for this fix only: the movement units under race, the Ethereum seam
  tests, vet, and the four named tests that plan a movement, all passing.
  The dust test plans none and its first-run pass stands. The evidence is in
  `implementation/K-07/local-2026-10-19/recheck-run`.
