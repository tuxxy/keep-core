## 3. Model scope (fixed before model code)

**Processes.** One host process stands for the honest signers of every
wallet (the signing quorum is abstracted as the worker's result). Process
crash and host crash at every host boundary. Every K-07 durable write goes
through the V-01 store commit, which syncs before it acknowledges, so at K-07
granularity a host crash loses only in-flight volatile state (A-V08-1, from
V-01). Restore of a stale store image is detected by the V-01 fence and
quarantines the store until the operator reconciles (A-V08-2, V-01
P-K02-DURABLE and assumption A-09).

**Wallets.** Source S (FROST, MovingFunds, with a main), committed target T
and alternative target A (both FROST, Live, no main; label T < label A).

**Moved outputs (bound "two moved outputs").** o1 is the movement's output
to its committed target; o2 is a drain output recorded to T by the
ECDSA-to-FROST route (S8). The source's one-movement bound: after a proven
movement the source makes no new commitment (a reopened source would create a
third moved output).

**Ticks.** Three. Timeouts (movement, incoming sweep) are enabled from tick 2;
challenge windows close at tick 3; the closing period is one tick.

**Chain actions (environment).** Commitment finalized or orphaned before
finality; target state change (C-09 `moveFunds`); movement mined and proven;
movement timeout (escalation, still provable); drain output recorded;
incoming record finalized; incoming timeout (escalation); sweep mined; sweep
proven with or without a lost reply, and resubmitted by anyone; keeper
`finalize`; late credit to a Closing or Closed wallet (a proven late deposit
sweep, S12); challenge opened and finalized.

**Host actions.** Commit (leader); movement plan, durable claim (one attempt
per round), grant (authority recheck plus executor prevout check), signature,
broadcast (from the live attempt or from the durable result after a crash,
with the authority recheck); incoming sweep plan (proposal trigger S4), claim,
grant, signature, broadcast; defense of a challenge; the `Closure` library's
finalized read, current reread, notify (with the lost-reply reread) and
archive; crash (two kinds); stale restore; reconcile.

**Not modeled (with owner).** Fee amounts and the equal split (VF, Go tests);
the main lock and the supersede rule (V-07); redemption rows 32 and 64 (V-04);
dust and `notifyBelowDust` (D8/D17 keeper duty; the host part "no doomed
commitment" is covered by the batch-2 tests); reorganization of proofs and
incoming records (only the commitment is orphanable); Bitcoin mempool
eviction; quarantined targets and directives (V-04 containment); the
wall-clock safety margin and the unswept-deposit wait (S13); coordination
leader and seat selection (D2, K-09 harness); several sources.

## 4. Predicates, weakenings and witnesses

The five predicates are fixed by KC-VER rev 02. Model encodings:

| Predicate | Invariant in the model |
| --- | --- |
| P-K07-COMMITTED-TARGET-ONLY | A movement grant is issued only when the current commitment is recorded, finalized, unproven and names the planned target; an incoming output is `Swept` only if a mined sweep spent it. |
| P-K07-INCOMING-ONCE | Each moved output is credited at most once. |
| P-K07-NO-STRANDED-MOVEMENT | An output in `Pending` or `Escalated` has a target the contract lets sweep (not `Closed`); a `Pending` output's target also passes the host's sweep filter and keeps its key; an unproven, finalized commitment has a source the host can still sign for. (`settled` / `pending` / `escalated` are then exhaustive.) |
| P-K07-CLOSE-ONLY-WHEN-CLEAR | The host sends a closure notice only after its own current-head read of the full ledger was empty (a late arrival after that read is left to the C-09 recheck). |
| P-K07-SIGNER-RETENTION | A key is absent only for a `Closed` wallet with an empty ledger, every challenge window final (tick 3) and no open challenge. |

Weakenings (R-07), each expected to break exactly its predicate:

| Weakening | Removes | Breaks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `TargetsFromCurrentEligibility` (KC-VER) | plan and authority take the lowest eligible target now instead of the commitment | COMMITTED-TARGET-ONLY |
| `PlanTimeCommitmentOnly` | the authority recheck at the grant | COMMITTED-TARGET-ONLY |
| `ForgetIncomingKey` (KC-VER) | the C-06 consumption key (`state` and `spentMainUTXOs`) on a sweep proof | INCOMING-ONCE |
| `CloseWithIncomingPending` (KC-VER) | row 2 from the C-09 obligations (state change, finalize, host read) | NO-STRANDED-MOVEMENT |
| `SweepOnlyWhenLive` | the FROST sweep state filter, restoring a Live-only filter | NO-STRANDED-MOVEMENT |
| `MainFieldOnly` (KC-VER) | every ledger row except custody from the host's reads | CLOSE-ONLY-WHEN-CLEAR |
| `NoRecheckBeforeNotify` | the current-head reread | CLOSE-ONLY-WHEN-CLEAR |
| `DeleteKeyAtClosing` (KC-VER) | retention (deletes the key when the wallet is `Closing`) | SIGNER-RETENTION |

Witnesses (`sometimes`):

- W-movement-completes-target-ineligible (KC-VER): a movement is proven after its target left the eligible states.
- W-sweep-after-lost-reply-credits-once (KC-VER): a sweep proof with a lost reply, outputs credited once.
- W-escalated-recovered (KC-VER "the existing stranded case and its specified recovery", FROST form): an escalated output is swept by its target.
- W-late-arrival-reopens (KC-VER "a late deposit after the check is still sweepable"): a late credit reopens a `Closing` or `Closed` wallet.
- W-defense-after-closure-begins (KC-VER): a challenge defended while the wallet is `Closing` or `Closed`.
- W-drain-swept (BOUNDARIES): the drain output is swept by the FROST target.
- W-closed-and-archived: the closure library archives a `Closed` wallet.
- W-recommit-after-orphan: a movement is proven after its first commitment was orphaned.
- **W-escalated-only-recovered — proposed, not in KC-VER.** An escalated output is swept by a sweep none of whose inputs is `Pending`. Reason: `BOUNDARIES.md` reading 1 and the C-06 comment ("It stays sweepable by the target and keeps blocking its closure") make the target's own sweep the recovery of an escalated output; KC-VER's witness does not say whether a `Pending` companion may be required. The claim model is run with this witness; if it is unreachable the result is a finding, not a predicate failure.

A probe run `ProposeEscalated` (not a weakening, never cited as a result)
lets the proposal trigger on an `Escalated` output, to show what the
proposed witness needs and that safety still holds.

## 5. Replay plan

Replay runs in `pkg/frost/movement` as an in-package test (it needs the
unexported hooks and the existing controlled chain `fake`). It replays on
the real code: `Commitments.Commit`, `Planner.Movement`,
`Planner.IncomingSweep`, `Authority.AuthorizeSweep` (grant and broadcast
recheck), `Closure.Obligations` and `Closure.Close` (with the
`before-closure-notify` hook to interleave the environment). Environment
steps are applied to the controlled chain. Steps without a reachable real
seam (durable claim, worker signature, executor prevout check, broadcast,
crash, restore, defense, maintainer) are applied as modeled; a trace with
any such step counts as partial. A trace is skipped when it needs a
contract-side weakening.
